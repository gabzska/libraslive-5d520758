# Architecture rules

- Use browser-observed platform status for connection and avatar indicators; never infer AI availability from network connectivity.
- Keep translation confidence assessment in the shared browser-safe helper; sentence fluency must not conceal uncertain sign recognition.
- Personal corrections retain original confidence and are labeled separately; they are not professional validation or global model training.
- Library publication approval and source attribution are not evidence of review by a fluent signer; do not label them as such.
